## Limits of this note
- Susceptibility comes from terrain only (SRTM, 30 m) and a small, mixed-quality inventory.
- Rainfall is CHIRPS (about 5 km), so neighbouring segments often share one value.
- Geology, soil depth and NDVI change are not included in this version.
- Risk classes use an expert-judgement matrix that is not calibrated on events.
